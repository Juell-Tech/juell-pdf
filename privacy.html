Juell PDF privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Juell PDF is made by Juell-Tech. This policy covers the Juell PDF app for Windows and the Juell PDF Reader app for Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Juell PDF has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your documents, signatures and settings stay on your PC and are never sent to Juell-Tech or anyone else.

Internet use

Juell PDF works offline. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to add a trusted timestamp or long-term validation data to a digital signature: it then contacts the timestamp service and your certificate issuer's servers to obtain them. Documents you send by email or WhatsApp go through those apps, under their own privacy policies.

Juell PDF Reader for Android

Juell PDF Reader collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't share any data with Juell-Tech or anyone else.

Documents are opened on your device only. When another app hands a PDF to Juell PDF Reader ("Open with" or Share), a copy is kept in the app's private storage so it can be shown; only the most recent one is kept, and it is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

The app doesn't connect to the internet by itself. Links in a document, or in the app's Help, open in your browser or email app only when you tap them, under those apps' own privacy policies.
